Three things to do before you arrive

  1. Register your vehicle

    Parking is free, but your vehicle must be registered to avoid a parking charge. Registering lets you park in the conference centre car park and in marked bays across the campus.

    Register your vehicle

    Can't register in advance? There are QR codes in the conference centre, but you must register within one hour of arriving.

  2. Get ready for your workbook

    You will fill in a workbook across the two days. Bring these with you, or have a think about them beforehand. You can read the workbook on this page.

    What to bring or think about

    Knowing my patch

    • Number of 3-star Accredited clubs
    • Number of 2-star Accredited clubs
    • Club Development Reviews completed
    • Club Development Reviews outstanding
    • Which pillars provide the greatest opportunity and progression for clubs?
    • Which pillars are the biggest barriers for clubs?

    Teamwork and collaboration

    • Who are my FA Club Consultants?
    • Do we need to recruit additional FA Club Consultants?
    • Who do I need to get closer to in my County FA, in the network and across stakeholders?

    Development mindset

    • What are my strengths?
    • Where can I improve?
    • Who can I engage with to improve my knowledge or fill any gaps?

People

Charlotte Richardson

Sports broadcaster and conference host

Charlotte is a freelance presenter, reporter and football commentator who became talkSPORT's first female commentator and covers the whole football pyramid on radio and TV. Her broadcasting career began in BBC local radio before she was chosen to report for Final Score, and she is a regular voice on Times Radio Breakfast and a pitch-side presenter and live host for Charlton TV at The Valley.

More about Charlotte

Grassroots football runs through her work. She is an FA Club Consultant and travels the country presenting the FA Club Hub podcast, In The Box, and before moving into broadcasting she worked in marketing and communications at Gillingham FC and Kent FA. In 2016 she founded Eighth Wonder, a programme to develop football's future female leaders, aimed at 14 to 20 year olds, which has helped more than 35 women into coaching.

Sue Day MBE

Director of Women's Football, The FA

Keynote: Leading the Orchestra. Day 2, 11.10am, Main Room 1.

Sue joined The FA in December 2024 as Director of Women's Football, where she is responsible for the next phase of development for the women's and girls' game and disability football in England, including delivery of the 2024-28 women's strategy.

More about Sue

She previously spent six years on the board of the Rugby Football Union as Chief Operations Officer and Chief Financial Officer, where she played a leading role in establishing Premiership Women's Rugby as an independent league and in England's successful bid to host the 2025 Women's Rugby World Cup. A chartered accountant, she spent over 20 years at KPMG, becoming a partner in 2015.

A former England captain, Sue won 59 caps between 1997 and 2007, played in three Rugby World Cups, won three Grand Slams and remains England women's record try-scorer with 61 tries. She was the first female President of Wasps FC and is a founding trustee of the Women's Sport Trust. She was awarded an MBE in 2020 for services to gender equality in sport and was named in the World Rugby Hall of Fame class of 2026.

Travel and hotel

Arriving by car

Parking is free, but your vehicle must be registered. The cameras on University Park Campus are managed by a third-party provider, so please register before you arrive to avoid any parking charges.

Registering lets you park in the conference centre's car park and in marked bays across the campus. EV charging points are available on site.

Register your vehicle

Travelling by train

Nottingham and Beeston stations are both a short taxi ride from the venue, and the Nottingham tram network also stops nearby.

Staying overnight

If you requested a room for Tuesday 20 October, you are staying at The Orchard Hotel, right next door to the conference centre.

  • Check in at the end of Day 1, from 4.30pm.
  • You can store your luggage at the hotel when you arrive, or leave it in your car.
  • Breakfast is served in Bramleys, the hotel restaurant, from 7.00am.
  • Check out before Day 2 starts. Registration is from 9.00am.

Food and drink

There is food and drink throughout the two days, and the venue knows about every dietary requirement registered with us.

On Tuesday evening we provide food and soft drinks. Alcoholic drinks are available to buy. They can't be charged to your room unless you give a payment card when you check in.

What to wear

Wear whatever is comfortable for the conference. The evening is smart casual.

Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel and the conference centre. We will share the details on the day.

Gym, pool and leisure

Hotel guests can use the gym and swimming pool at the David Ross Sports Village, across from The Orchard Hotel, free of charge and subject to availability. The hotel also has its own small gym on the ground floor.

  • Open daily from 6.30am to 10.30pm.
  • Book your gym or swim session through the hotel reception team.
  • Squash, racquetball, badminton, the climbing wall, snooker, and the sauna and steam room can also be booked. These cost £6.50 per person, per activity off peak (6.30am to 3pm) and £7.50 at peak times (3pm to 11pm).
